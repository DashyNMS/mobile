# DashyNMS Mobile user guide

<img src="https://dashynms.pckp.net/assets/img/favicon.svg" alt="DashyNMS" width="80" height="80">

**Your LibreNMS network, in your pocket.**

See what's down, find out why an alert fired and look into a device from wherever you are. This guide walks you through the jobs you do in DashyNMS on a phone or tablet.

[Website](https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/) · [Help and support](https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/support/) · [Report a problem](https://github.com/DashyNMS/mobile/issues)

These pages cover DashyNMS Mobile 2.0. What you see depends on your LibreNMS permissions and on the data your server collects. A few buttons sit in a different place on iPhone, iPad and Android; the guide says so where it matters.

- [Install on iPhone and iPad](installation/install-iphone-ipad.md)

## Everyday tasks

- [Arrange your dashboard](how-to/dashboard.md): choose, order and set up its cards.
- [Add home screen and lock screen widgets](how-to/widgets.md): see the worst problems without opening the app.
- [Investigate and acknowledge alerts](how-to/alerts.md): find out why one fired and say you're on it.
- [Choose which alerts notify you](how-to/notifications.md): severities, rules and quiet hours.
- [Find and investigate a device](how-to/devices.md): search, pin and open every section of a device.
- [Check ports and graphs](how-to/ports-and-graphs.md): errors, traffic and changes over time.
- [Schedule maintenance and rediscover devices](how-to/maintenance.md): before and after planned work.
- [Read logs](how-to/logs.md): the event log, the alert log and Graylog.
- [Use maps, neighbours and health readings](how-to/maps-and-health.md): sites, links and sensors.
- [Find where something is connected](how-to/network-search.md): Network Search, by address, MAC or name.
- [Ask about your network with Intelligence](how-to/intelligence.md): answers from Apple Intelligence on your iPhone or iPad.
