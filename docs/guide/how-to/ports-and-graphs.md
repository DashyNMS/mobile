# Check ports and graphs

## Check a port

1. Open a device and tap **Ports**.
2. Search for the port by name or description, or open **Sort & filter** to sort or group the list.
3. Tap a port for its traffic, packet and error graphs.

A port's state, speed and traffic come from LibreNMS's last poll. A dash means LibreNMS had no reading, which is different from zero.

## Compare over time

1. Open a device and tap **Graphs**, or open a port.
2. Choose a graph under **Graph** and a time range.
3. Use **Show** to move between the device's own graphs and one of its ports. The time range is kept.

Graphs with several lines list their series. Tap a series to hide or show it, press and hold it to see **Only** that one, and choose **Show all** to bring the rest back. On a wide screen the list sits beside the graph.

If a graph is empty, try a longer range and check that LibreNMS collects it.

## Keep a graph or reading in view

Add a **Graph** or **Sensors** card to [your dashboard](dashboard.md#add-a-card).
