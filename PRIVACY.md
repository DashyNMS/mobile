# Privacy

DashyNMS Mobile doesn't collect, store or share any of your data. It talks only to the LibreNMS server you sign in to. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The full privacy policy is on the website: **<https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/privacy/>**. It's also linked in the app under Settings → About.

The website is the one official copy, so this file only points to it rather than repeating it.
